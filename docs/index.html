EasyInterview : AI Interview Assistant

Official Privacy Policy & Data Protection Disclosure
Effective Date: November 25, 2026 Verified Local-First Extension
🔒 100% Client-Side / Zero Cloud Storage Promise

EasyInterview operates entirely inside your local browser. We do not operate external databases, we do not record audio or video streams, we do not track your browsing history, and we never sell your personal or professional data to third parties.

1. Overview & Single Purpose

EasyInterview : AI Interview Assistant is a browser extension created to assist job applicants during live interviews on Google Meet. The extension's single purpose is to capture spoken interview questions in real time, correlate them with the candidate's resume and background, and provide clear, structured speaking points and narrative answers.

2. What Data Is Processed & How It Is Handled

A. Google Meet Live Transcripts & Closed Captions

  • Data Handled: Temporary text utterances generated by Google Meet's speech-to-text service during an active interview session.
  • Storage: Transient (In-Memory Only). The transcripts reside strictly in temporary volatile memory during the active meeting and are permanently discarded when the meeting tab is closed or reloaded.
  • Audio & Video: The extension does NOT record, transmit, or save any microphone audio or webcam video streams.

B. Candidate Profile & Resume (CV) Data

  • Data Handled: Target position, company name, and parsed resume text.
  • Local Extraction: Uploaded PDF and DOCX files are processed 100% locally in your browser memory using offline libraries (PDF.js and Mammoth). No resume document is ever uploaded to a remote file server.
  • Retention: Stored locally on your device in your browser's private storage partition (chrome.storage.local).

C. AI Credentials & API Keys

  • Data Handled: Personal API keys for third-party LLM providers (e.g., OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, Groq, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, or local Ollama).
  • Retention: Stored strictly on your local machine using chrome.storage.local. API keys are never transmitted to us or any analytics service.

3. Data Transmission to Third Parties

The Extension communicates over the network strictly upon your direct interaction and only to fulfill its core functionality:

  • Your Selected AI Provider: When an interview question is detected or when you trigger an answer, the Extension sends an HTTPS request containing the question, recent transcript context, and your resume facts directly to your chosen AI model provider. Your API key is included solely in the secure HTTP authorization header directly to that provider.
  • Update Checks: The Extension queries GitHub Releases to determine if a newer extension release is available. No personal data or credentials are included in update queries.

4. Permissions Disclosure & Least Privilege

In accordance with Google Chrome Web Store Developer Policies, EasyInterview requests only the permissions essential for operation:

Permission Technical Justification
storage Used to save your resume text, settings, and widget position locally on your device via chrome.storage.local.
activeTab / tabs Used by the background service worker to check if the active tab is Google Meet before executing keyboard shortcuts (Alt+H to toggle HUD, Alt+J to Answer Now).
https://meet.google.com/* Required to listen to closed captions and render the floating HUD overlay on Google Meet.
host_permissions Required to transmit prompt completion requests directly to your chosen AI model provider or custom self-hosted endpoint.

5. User Control & Data Deletion

  • Instant Wipe: You can purge all stored context, resume texts, and API keys at any time by clicking "Reset Defaults" in the Extension Popup.
  • Uninstall: Removing the extension from Chrome permanently deletes all associated data from chrome.storage.local.
