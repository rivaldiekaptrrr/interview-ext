Privacy Policy

EasyInterview : AI Interview Assistant • Effective Date: November 25, 2026
Zero Cloud Storage Promise: EasyInterview operates under a 100% Client-Side Local-First architecture. We do not operate databases, we do not record audio/video, and we never track or sell your personal information.

1. Overview & Single Purpose

EasyInterview is designed to assist job seekers during live interviews on Google Meet by transcribing spoken questions and generating structured answer suggestions based on candidate resumes.

2. Data Processing & Retention

A. Google Meet Closed Captions

Transcripts are processed in-memory only during your active call session. No audio files or transcripts are ever permanently stored on any remote server.

B. Candidate Profile & Resume Data

Resume files (.pdf and .docx) are parsed 100% locally in your browser memory. Extracted resume facts are stored strictly on your computer using chrome.storage.local.

C. API Credentials

Personal API keys are stored solely on your local device and sent directly to your selected AI provider via secure HTTPS.

3. Permissions Justification

Permission Justification
storage To store resume context and configuration locally on your computer.
activeTab / tabs To route keyboard shortcuts and display the floating HUD on Google Meet tabs.
meet.google.com To capture live captions and display the interview assistant widget.
host_permissions To send completion prompts directly to your chosen AI model provider.

4. Data Deletion

You can instantly delete all stored context and API keys at any time by clicking "Reset Defaults" in the Extension Popup or by uninstalling the extension.
